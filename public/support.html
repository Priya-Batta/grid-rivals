Support and Complaints

Grid Rivals
Last updated: 13 November 2026

Support contact: gridrivals.support@gmail.com

How to contact us

Email gridrivals.support@gmail.com for gameplay problems, account or progress issues, privacy requests, safety concerns, suspected cheating, security reports or complaints about the service.

What to include

Describe the issue, when it happened, the page or feature involved, and any error message. Include your in-game display name if relevant. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. You may attach screenshots after checking that they do not expose private information.

Complaints and grievance handling

We will review complaints sent to the support address, assess the issue and respond using the contact details provided where a response is possible. If your complaint concerns privacy, a suspected security incident, player safety or an age-related concern, make that clear in the subject line so it can be prioritised. If you are dissatisfied with the response, reply to the same email and ask for the complaint to be escalated for further review.

We are preparing this process for the requirements applicable to online social games in India. The operator details, designated grievance contact, response timelines and any required escalation route must be confirmed before this page is treated as the final statutory grievance notice.

Privacy and data requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, email us from an address where we can reply and provide enough information to identify the relevant anonymous account. Account recovery may not be possible if the browser session has been lost.

Security reports

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to gridrivals.support@gmail.com. Do not publicly share details that could put players or the service at risk.
