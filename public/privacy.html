Privacy Policy

Grid Rivals
Last updated: 13 November 2026

Launch draft: This policy describes the current intended operation of Grid Rivals and must be checked against the live service and applicable legal requirements before public launch. It is not legal advice.

1. Who operates Grid Rivals

Grid Rivals is an independently operated online game. For privacy questions or requests, contact gridrivals.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

When you use the game, we may process an anonymous Supabase account identifier; the display name you choose; game sessions, scores and results; leaderboard entries; and progression information such as levels, achievements and daily challenge activity. The game also uses browser storage for settings and device-specific progress, such as sound preferences and locally stored best scores.

Do not include sensitive or unnecessary personal information in your display name or in messages to support.

3. How we use information

We use this information to operate the game, authenticate anonymous sessions, verify game results, maintain leaderboards, save progression, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems and respond to support requests.

4. Public leaderboard

If you participate in the leaderboard, your chosen display name, score, game mode or difficulty, player level and result date may be visible to other players. Do not use your real name or other identifying information as your display name.

5. Service providers

The current service uses Supabase for authentication, database and server-side game functionality, and Cloudflare Pages for website hosting. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy documentation.

6. Cookies and browser storage

Grid Rivals uses browser storage to keep an anonymous sign-in session and certain game preferences or device-specific progress. Clearing browser data may make an anonymous account inaccessible because account linking and account recovery are not currently available.

7. Advertising and analytics

The application code reviewed for this draft did not show an integrated third-party advertising or analytics provider. This statement must be updated if such services are added.

8. Retention and deletion requests

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the game, maintain records, address abuse or security issues, and meet applicable obligations. Actual retention may also depend on service-provider settings and requirements. To request access, correction or deletion, email gridrivals.support@gmail.com from an address where we can reply. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant anonymous account, and we may be unable to recover an account if its browser session has been lost.

9. Children's privacy and age policy

Grid Rivals' current intended access policy is 18 years or older for players in India. Outside India, the intended minimum age is 13, subject to any higher minimum age, parental-consent requirement or other restriction under local law. This statement is not a guarantee that every person aged 13–17 may use the game in every country. The game does not currently provide a verified parental-consent process. Access controls must be implemented and tested before relying on this policy.

10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including server-side verification of game results and database access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please report suspected vulnerabilities or security incidents to gridrivals.support@gmail.com.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact gridrivals.support@gmail.com.
